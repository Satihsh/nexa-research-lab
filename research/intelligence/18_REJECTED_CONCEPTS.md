# 18: REJECTED CONCEPTS

**Scope and rule:**
- These are **concepts rejected for NEXA in this phase**, each with a reason.
- **Rejection is not a claim that the idea is false in general.** "Data" rejections are re-opened if data is acquired.
- The original `FAILED_IDEAS.md` §E–F remain in force. These are additions.

| # | Concept | Source | Reason | Re-open if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Hard-coded "put wall = support / call wall = resistance"** | S53, S54 (as convention) | Untested in every source. The direction depends on the sign assumption (CN1) and on HPR (CN2). Only conditional probabilities are admissible (05) | Never as a hard rule |
| R2 | **GEX / walls / flip / pins for EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY** | S53, S54 | FX options are mostly OTC: no public OI. CME FX options are not in Massive. ETF chains are thin | Reliable OI + IV per strike becomes obtainable |
